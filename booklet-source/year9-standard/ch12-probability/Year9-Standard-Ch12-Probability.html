In this chapter you will

  • find the probability of an event and of its complement
  • find observed (experimental) probabilities and compare them with theoretical probabilities
  • use Venn diagrams to find probabilities with "and", "or" and "neither"
  • list the outcomes of two-step experiments with tree diagrams
  • use tables of outcomes for two dice or spinners
  • write probabilities as fractions in simplest form, decimals or percentages

About the artwork

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, thousands of times for every point. Every booklet shows a different part of the set: this one is a silver jellyfish of spirals near the neck of the left bulb, coloured in purple and silver. Maths can make beautiful things.
