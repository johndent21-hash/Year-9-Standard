In this chapter you will

  • convert between metric units, including very large and very small units
  • find the precision, absolute error and percentage error of a measurement
  • find the perimeter and area of composite shapes and quadrilaterals
  • find the circumference, arc length and area of circles, sectors and composite shapes
  • find the surface area of prisms and cylinders
  • find the volume and capacity of prisms and cylinders

About the artwork

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, thousands of times for every point. Every booklet shows a different part of the set: this one is a silver spiral on the western edge of Seahorse Valley, coloured in purple and silver. Maths can make beautiful things.
