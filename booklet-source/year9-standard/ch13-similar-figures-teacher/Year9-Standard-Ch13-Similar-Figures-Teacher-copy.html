In this chapter you will

  • name the matching sides and angles of similar figures and write a similarity statement (ABC ||| PQR)
  • find the scale factor of a pair of similar figures
  • draw enlargements and reductions on a grid
  • test whether two figures are similar
  • find unknown sides of similar figures
  • use shadows, scale drawings and maps to find real heights and lengths

About the artwork

The pictures in this booklet are parts of the Mandelbrot set, drawn by a computer that repeats one simple rule, z → z² + c, thousands of times for every point. Every booklet shows a different part of the set: this one is a small copy of the whole Mandelbrot set on its antenna (the set is self-similar), coloured in purple and silver. Maths can make beautiful things.
