## Saving maths resources to Google Drive

Whenever you finish making a maths resource (booklet, help sheet, answer key, test, worksheet and so on), **ask the user whether they want it sent to their Google Drive** before uploading anything.

If they say yes, upload it into the matching subfolder of the **Kingscliff Maths Resources 7–12** folder (Drive folder id `1kJSCCR68EnUdiTqjzADZv-pyT8nUsgsj`). Pick the year folder, then the resource-type folder inside it. Examples:

- Year 9 yearly exam material → `Year 09 / Yearly Exams`
- Year 9 chapter booklets → `Year 09 / Chapter Booklets`
- Year 9 warm-ups → `Year 09 / Warm-ups – The Fractal Five`
- Year 7 revision booklets → `Year 07 / Revision Booklets`

If no subfolder fits, suggest one and confirm it with the user before creating it.
